## Why "prices drop every minute"
- A real "wait, really?" core: Filene's Basement (Boston, 1909) cut prices automatically by days on sale
  and gave the unsold items to charity; Dutch flower auctions sell with a falling price clock.
- Built-in tension (who buys first?), a villain everyone hates (a reseller bot), a small human goal (Theo's $12 robot
  for his sister), a running joke (Mia's ugly sweater, "not yet"), a cost at the climax ($40 short) and a warm payoff.
- Fiction: no real store or brand; the shop is Grandma Penny's.

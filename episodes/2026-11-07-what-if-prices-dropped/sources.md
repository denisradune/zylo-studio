# Sources — What if prices dropped every minute you waited?

- Wikipedia, "Filene's Basement" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filene%27s_Basement
  - Store opened in 1909 as the "Automatic Bargain Basement" (idea conceived by Edward A. Filene in 1908).
  - 1982 NYT description quoted: "every article is marked with a tag showing the price and the date the article was
    first put on sale." "Twelve days later, if it has not been sold, it is reduced by 25 percent." "Six selling days
    later, it is cut by 50 percent." then "75 percent off". "After six more days—or a total of 30—if it is not sold,
    it is given to charity."
  - Used: Boston 1909, falling prices, 12 days → −25%, then −50%, −75%, then charity.
- Wikipedia, "Dutch auction" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_auction
  - A Dutch auction "begins with a high offer price" and "lowers it until some participant accepts the price."
  - Dutch flower auctions "use an electronic device to lower the price", shown "on a clock".
  - Used: price starts high, falls until someone says yes; flower markets with a falling clock.
- Black Friday 2026 = Friday, November 27 — https://www.sofi.com/learn/content/when-is-black-friday/

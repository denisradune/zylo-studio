## Decision (rule 1a.2 — follow up the HIT's angle, not its disaster theme)
New premise in the same angle as the hit: a real creature's superpower, copied by Bit, with a twist.
Theme: sci-fi twist (real nature) — new on the channel, lighter/warmer than disaster.

Candidates considered:
1. Sea slug that steals chloroplasts and lives on light (Elysia chlorotica) — "eat sunlight", huge relatable wish
   (free food), strong visual (people turn green), built-in catch (light alone can't make them grow). **Chosen.**
2. Axolotl regeneration — fun, but body-horror risk for animation.
3. Tardigrades surviving space — overused topic on Shorts.
4. Bears hibernating — weaker twist.

Story beats: broke Jay → Bit's sea-slug smoothie → free lunch goes viral → 14 days of clouds → the catch (light helps,
doesn't replace food; Bit's code only switched off the feeling) → Rosa's soup is the cure and the real reason
(Jay was too proud to accept free lunches) → the city cooks a million bowls, Bit's battery is the cost → sun returns,
Jay hired, a leaf sprouts on his head. Closed ending ("Follow for a new What if every day").

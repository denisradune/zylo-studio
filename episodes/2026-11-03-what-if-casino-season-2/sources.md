# Sources — Casino Season 2

Fiction. Real-world claims used in the script, each checked 2026-11-03:

1. Martingale (parts 2–3): double after every loss; one win recovers all losses + the first stake; only unbounded
   money wins; bet limits defeat it.
   - Wikipedia, "Martingale (betting system)": "The strategy had the gambler double the bet after every loss";
     "It is only with unbounded wealth, bets and time that it could be argued that the martingale becomes a winning
     strategy"; "The martingale strategy fails even with unbounded stopping time, as long as there is a limit on
     earnings or on the bets". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_(betting_system)
   - Arithmetic in part 3: 1+2+4+…+128 = 255 (thousand) lost over 8 blacks; the next double (256K) exceeds a 100K limit.
2. Table limits (part 3): Wikipedia, "Roulette": "The roulette table usually imposes minimum and maximum bets".
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roulette
3. Biased wheels (part 4): Wikipedia, "Roulette": schemes "work by determining that the ball is more likely to fall
   at certain numbers"; Richard Jarecki "observed more than 10,000 spins of each roulette wheel to determine flaws
   in the wheels". (Script: "Real players have watched wheels for ten thousand spins." — no name used.)
4. Monte Carlo 1913 (part 6): Wikipedia, "Gambler's fallacy": the ball "fell in black 26 times in a row";
   "Gamblers lost millions of francs betting against black". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambler%27s_fallacy
5. Payout 35 to 1 on a single number: as in season 1 sources (Wikipedia, "Roulette").
Not used: any claim that casinos hide clocks (disputed, cut in season 1).

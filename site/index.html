FAQ

Questions people ask us

How often do new videos come out?

Every day. A new Short goes up daily, and a longer full story comes out every week. New videos show up on this page automatically.

Are the facts real?

Yes. The stories are fiction, but the facts inside them are not. Every fact is checked against published, reputable sources before it goes in a script. If we can't confirm a claim, it doesn't make it into the video.

Can my idea become a video?

Yes. Ideas sent through the form go straight to the person who runs the channel, and the best ones become new videos. Leave your email if you want to know when yours is out.

How are the videos made?

zylo uses AI tools for research, voice-over, music and animation. A person picks the topics, checks the facts and sets the direction of the channel. The characters are original cartoons, not real people.

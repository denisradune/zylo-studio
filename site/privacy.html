Privacy

zylo.video collects as little as possible. Here is exactly what we keep, why, and how to get it removed. Last updated: November 23, 2026.

Video ideas

When you send an idea we store the text, the rubric you picked and the time. Your name and email are optional and stored only if you type them in.

We use them to read your idea and, if you left an email, to tell you when it becomes a video. We never sell them, share them for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Questions to “Ask zylo”

Your question and the answer are stored so we can see what people are curious about and turn the best questions into new Shorts.

To answer instantly, the question is sent to an AI provider (Anthropic), which processes it to write the reply. Please don't put personal details in your questions.

Videos

Videos play from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Thumbnails load from YouTube's image servers. When you play a video or open it on YouTube, Google's privacy policy applies.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) for security and to block spam. We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Delete your data

Want your idea, name or email removed? Send a message through the Suggest a video form, choose “Something else”, write “Delete my data” and include the email you used before. We delete it within 30 days.

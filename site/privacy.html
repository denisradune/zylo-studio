Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

  • Video ideas. When you send an idea we store the text and, only if you enter them, your name and email. We use them to read your idea and to tell you if it becomes a video. We never sell them or use them for ads.
  • Questions. Questions you ask are stored so we can improve answers and pick topics for new videos. If instant answers are on, your question is sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the reply. Don't include personal details in questions.
  • Videos. Videos play from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's own privacy policy applies when you watch or open a video on YouTube.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address) for security.
  • Deleting your data. Want your idea or email removed? Use the idea form to ask, include the email you used, and we'll delete it.
